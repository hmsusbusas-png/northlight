FAQ

Questions, answered

How is Northlight different from Google Analytics?

Google Analytics measures traffic. Northlight measures product behaviour — events, funnels and cohorts tied to accounts and revenue, so the numbers match your billing.

How long does setup take?

Most teams are live in under an hour: drop in the SDK snippet, define your first two events, and historical backfill starts immediately.

Can I import data from another tool?

Yes. We import event history from Mixpanel, Amplitude and Segment during onboarding, so your reports are useful on day one.

Where is my data stored?

In the EU (Frankfurt) or US (Virginia) region of your choice, encrypted at rest. We never sell or share event data, and you can export or delete everything at any time.

What happens after the beta?

Beta workspaces keep 12 months of free usage. When we launch, you pick a plan or export your data — no lock-in either way.
